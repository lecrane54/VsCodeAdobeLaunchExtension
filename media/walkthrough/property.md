## Pick the property you work in

A **property** is one Launch container — its rules, data elements, and
extensions. Ids look like `PR8fb5f4098b86443b85d28c1ff98ba3f6`.

The picker lists every property your credential can see, and filters as you
type on name, platform, id, or domain.

Your choice is saved to workspace settings as `adobeLaunch.activePropertyId`,
so each project can point at a different property.

> Switching property clears the active library — libraries belong to a
> property, so a carried-over selection would target the wrong place.
